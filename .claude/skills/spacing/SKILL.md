# Spacing — Safar

## Scale (px)
4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64 · 96 · 128

Pick the nearest step. Odd values (9, 17, 19, 27, 34…) already in the CSS are legacy;
snap them to the scale when you edit that rule.

## Rules
- Section padding: 96–128 desktop, 64 tablet, 48 mobile (top and bottom).
- Gap between heading and its lead: 12–16. Lead to CTA: 24–32.
- Card inner padding: 24 desktop, 16 mobile.
- Grid gaps: 24 desktop, 16 mobile.
- Side gutter on mobile: 16–20, never less.
- Touch targets: at least 44×44 on mobile, including the menu button and toggles.
